# Google Productivity Workflow

A streamlined approach using **Google Keep** for capture and **Google Tasks** for action, integrated with the **Eisenhower Matrix** for prioritization.

## Core Philosophy: Capture vs. Action

### Google Keep: Universal Capture
Fast, friction-free capture of anything that enters your mind:
- Ideas and thoughts
- Voice memos  
- Photos of things to remember
- Quick reminders

:::tip Golden Rule
> Your brain is for *having* ideas, not for *holding* them. Capture everything in Keep without organizing.
:::

### Google Tasks: Action Engine
Where commitments live. Simple, structured task management with **seamless Google Calendar integration**.

## The 4 Quadrants Setup

Four dedicated lists in Google Tasks based on the Eisenhower Matrix:

1. **`1 - Do (Urgent & Important)`** - Crises, pressing deadlines
2. **`2 - Schedule (Important & Not Urgent)`** - Growth activities  
3. **`3 - Delegate (Urgent & Not Important)`** - Quick actions to offload
4. **`4 - Someday/Maybe (Not Important & Not Urgent)`** - Future considerations

## Daily Workflow

### Part A: Capture (Always-On)
Immediately capture any idea, thought, or task in Google Keep. No categorization—just pure capture.

### Part B: Process (Daily 15-Minute Review)
1. **Open Keep and Tasks side-by-side**
2. **For each Keep note, ask:**
   - **"Is this Important?"** (Aligns with goals/values?)
   - **"Is this Urgent?"** (Pressing deadline?)
3. **Take action:**
   - **Not actionable** → Archive in Keep with labels
   - **Actionable** → Create task in appropriate quadrant, archive Keep note

## Calendar Integration

Tasks automatically appear in Google Calendar:

- **Q1 (Do):** Set due date to today, drag into time slots
- **Q2 (Schedule):** Set future due dates, time-block proactively  
- **Q3 (Delegate):** Today's date, quick 15-minute blocks
- **Q4 (Eliminate):** Don't schedule—delete or postpone indefinitely

## Starred Tasks: Cross-Quadrant Focus

**Starred tasks** represent your current focus items across all quadrants. This creates a dynamic "focus list" that transcends the rigid quadrant structure:

- **Star tasks** you're actively working on, regardless of quadrant
- **Maximum 3-5 starred tasks** to maintain focus
- **iOS widget integration** displays starred tasks on your home screen
- **Quick access** to your most important current work
- **Regular review** to star/unstar as priorities shift

:::tip Focus Strategy
Use stars for tasks you want to see immediately when opening your phone—your true daily priorities.
:::

## Key Advantages

- **Frictionless capture** with Keep's speed
- **Clear priorities** through 4-quadrant decision framework
- **Visual planning** with Calendar drag-and-drop
- **Ecosystem leverage** using existing Google tools
- **Built-in accountability** with automated Calendar integration

## Quick Start

**Days 1-2:** Create task lists, install Keep, practice rapid capture  
**Days 3-4:** Establish daily processing routine  
**Days 5-7:** Begin Calendar time-blocking

:::tip Success Metric
After one week: noticeable reduction in mental load and increased daily clarity.
:::

This systematic approach transforms productivity from reactive scrambling to proactive, intentional action using your existing Google ecosystem.
