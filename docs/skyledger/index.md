Skyledger is my curated knowledge base for cloud foundation and infrastructure, designed to support enterprise environments within the European Economic Area (EEA). This section brings together the lessons, guidelines, and architectural decisions I’ve gathered through hands-on experience and research in cloud strategy.

Here, you’ll find implementation recommendations, best practices, and actionable advice for building robust, compliant, and scalable cloud platforms. I also highlight common pitfalls to avoid, with a special focus on the unique challenges faced by enterprises operating in the EEA.

This is a living document, continuously updated as technology evolves and new insights emerge. My aim is to provide clear, practical guidance for anyone working to establish or improve cloud infrastructure in complex organizational settings.
